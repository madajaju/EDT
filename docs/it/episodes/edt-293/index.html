Episode 293 Put the title Right Here

2025-09-30T13:09:40.159Z Hosted by Dr. Darren Pulsipher

Explore more in the episode archive.

Summary

Dr. Darren welcomes Alister Frost, a seasoned change evangelist and author, to discuss the pivotal themes of Change, obsolescence, and personal missions in the workplace. Frost shares his unique journey from working in manufacturing at Kimberly Clark to the tech world at Microsoft, emphasizing how his experiences shaped his understanding of the essential need for continuous improvement and adaptation in an ever-changing landscape. Throughout the episode, Frost reveals his framework, known as the "FROST model," which outlines how individuals and organizations can adopt a culture of Change through openness, curiosity, and mission-driven work. This episode is packed with valuable insights tailored for technologists and business leaders who are eager to navigate changes effectively while fostering innovation within their organizations. ## Takeaways - Change is inevitable; embracing it can lead to growth and innovation. - Personal missions in the workplace can energize employees and lead to meaningful improvements. - The "FROST model" encourages individuals to question the status quo and seek new perspectives. - Leaders should create environments where employees feel empowered to suggest and implement changes. - Continuous learning and adaptability are crucial in today's fast-paced work environment. ## Chapters - [00:00] Introduction to Dr. Darren and Alister Frost - [02:30] Alister's Origin Story: From Manufacturing to Microsoft - [05:15] The Shift in Mindset: From Stability to Change - [10:00] The Importance of Personal Mission - [15:30] The FROST Model: Overview of the Framework - [20:45] How to Foster a Culture of Change in Organizations - [25:00] Dealing with Resistance to Change - [30:00] The Role of Curiosity in Driving Change
