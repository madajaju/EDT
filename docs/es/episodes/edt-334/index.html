Episode 334 Put the title Right Here

2026-03-17 Hosted by Dr. Darren Pulsipher

Explore more in the episode archive.

Summary

Privacy and data security are no longer optional; they're essential. In this episode of Embracing Digital Transformation, host Dr. Darren sits down with John Byron Hanby IV, CEO of Iternal Technologies, to explore the intersection of AI, privacy, and the overwhelming demand for data. Hanby shares his unique journey from filmmaker to tech entrepreneur, shedding light on the rapid evolution of generative AI and the risks of data exposure in today's digital landscape. ## Key Takeaways: - **Data Privacy is Crucial**: With large AI model developers craving more data, ensuring your data's safety and privacy is paramount. - **Education is Key**: Organizations need to prioritize AI literacy to enhance employee efficiency and harness the full potential of generative AI tools. - **Open Source vs. Private AI**: While public AI models are highly capable, private AI solutions can be tailored for specific business needs with adequate training. - **Prompting Matters**: The effectiveness of AI implementations often depends on how well users can communicate their needs to the technology. - **Risk vs. Reward**: Balancing the potential benefits of using AI against the risks of exposing sensitive data is a critical conversation for modern enterprises. - **Community Matters**: Engaging with others in the digital transformation conversation enhances understanding and promotes innovative solutions. ## Chapters: - **00:00** - Introduction to Dr. Darren and John Byron Hanby - **03:15** - John's journey from filmmaking to AI entrepreneurship - **10:20** - The importance of privacy in an AI-driven world - **18:45** - AI adoption rates and the skepticism among users - **25:10** - Public vs. private generative AI: what you need to know - **32:00** - The significance of training and AI literacy in the workplace - **38:20** - Closing thoughts and where to find more about Iternal Technologies Listen to the full conversation and discover how to embrace digital transformation while safeguarding your data!
