Csaba Toth

Biography

Csaba Toth is the founder of ICQ Global and the creator of the Global DISC and Growth Zone models. His work focuses on helping organizations understand the human forces that shape team performance, including psychological safety, motivational drivers, cultural intelligence, and cognitive diversity. He works with leaders and teams that want to move beyond surface-level diversity or generic culture statements and develop a more accurate understanding of how people actually think, communicate, and make decisions. Csaba grew up between cultures and has worked across different organizations and environments. Those experiences led him to study the assumptions people bring into teams and the way those assumptions influence trust, conflict, leadership, and collaboration. Through ICQ Global, he has developed assessment and development approaches used by coaches, consultants, and organizations in multiple countries, supported by a network of licensed partners. His leadership work emphasizes practical self-awareness rather than labels. Csaba argues that culture, personality, and conditioning interact in complex ways, and that leaders need better tools for surfacing disagreement and building productive discomfort. He is also interested in how technology and AI can reinforce bias or create false consensus when teams use them to avoid human friction. Through ICQ Global, speaking, writing, and executive coaching, he helps organizations build teams that are more resilient, inclusive, and capable of making better decisions under pressure.
