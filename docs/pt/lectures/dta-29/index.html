Episode 29 Super Intelligence: Why Teams Need an AI-Augmented Operating System

2026-10-28 Hosted by Dr. Darren Pulsipher

Explore more in the episode archive.

Summary

In this episode, Dr. Darren explores the concept of super intelligence as a shared capability formed when people and AI collaborate within an AI-Augmented Operating System (AAOS). He outlines how organizations can move beyond isolated AI experiments to responsible, repeatable AI-enabled work by connecting AI outputs to accountable workflows. The episode explains the six-stage AAOS cycle—Diagnose, Activate, Control, Execute, Measure, and Scale—and emphasizes the importance of clear boundaries, ownership, evidence, and continuous learning to create dependable AI-augmented operations.
