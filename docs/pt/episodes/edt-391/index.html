Episode 391 Put the title Right Here

2026-10-06 Hosted by Dr. Darren Pulsipher

Explore more in the episode archive.

Summary

Regulated industries are under pressure to adopt AI without compromising security, compliance, or critical thinking. In this episode, Aaron Bach and Dr. Darren Pulsipher discuss why blanket AI bans fail, how shadow AI increases organizational risk, and what leaders can do to adopt AI responsibly.

The conversation explores AI sovereignty, multi-model strategies, sensitive-data redaction, observability, human validation, and practical ways regulated organizations can move faster without losing control. The central message is clear: successful enterprise AI requires governance that supports innovation while preserving human judgment.
