Episode 8 Measure AI Outcomes, Not AI Activity: Build a Better Dashboard

2026-11-12 Hosted by Dr. Darren Pulsipher

Explore more in the episode archive.

Summary

This episode focuses on the critical importance of measuring AI outcomes rather than just AI activity. It covers how to establish meaningful measurement sets that balance outcomes, control, flow, and learning metrics to truly gauge operating system improvements. Key themes include setting baselines, interpreting evidence thoughtfully, assigning ownership to metrics, and using measurements to enable actionable decisions rather than superficial reporting. Using a practical example, the episode encourages building dashboards that reveal capability improvements and system health, helping teams to decide whether to continue, revise, hold, or stop AI initiatives.
