Summary

# Engage Employees Like a Coach: Insights from Stephen Bear Are you aware that a staggering 79% of employees worldwide are disengaged or highly disengaged? In this episode of Embracing Digital Transformation, host Dr. Darren discusses the critical need for employee engagement with guest Stephen Bear, a seasoned expert in workforce engagement and author of the upcoming book “Psychology.” Get ready to uncover valuable strategies for turning your workforce into passionate contributors and building a thriving organizational culture. ## Key Takeaways: - **Understanding the Cost of Disengagement**: Recognizing that disengaged employees cost organizations significantly more than investing in their development is crucial. - **Coaching vs. Managing**: Shift your perspective from simply managing employees to coaching them, enabling a deeper level of connection and accountability in the workplace. - **The Five Components of Engagement**: Stephen introduces his “Forever Framework,” which emphasizes identity, belonging, contribution, recognition, and progress to help foster employee engagement. - **Tailored Engagement for Different Levels**: While engagement strategies apply to all employees, middle management plays a pivotal role in execution and must be nurtured to ensure effective coaching and development. - **Data-Driven Human Connections**: Leverage data to personalize the employee experience, focusing on soft skills that create meaningful connections and elevate organizational performance. ## Chapters: - 00:00 – Introduction to the episode and guest Stephen Bear - 02:30 – The alarming statistics on employee disengagement - 05:10 – Transitioning from managing to coaching employees - 08:00 – The cost implications of disengagement - 11:15 – Introducing the “Forever Framework” for engagement - 17:20 – The importance of middle management in coaching - 20:00 – Personal anecdotes from Stephen’s experience - 25:45 – Data-driven strategies for enhancing employee connections - 30:00 – Conclusion and call to action Discover how to empower your employees and drive a culture of engagement that benefits both individuals and organizations. Tune in now to find practical advice that can transform your workplace environment!
