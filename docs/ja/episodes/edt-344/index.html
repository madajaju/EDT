Episode 344 AI時代における人間関係の力

2026-04-21 Hosted by Dr. Darren Pulsipher

Explore more in the episode archive.

Summary

In today’s fast-evolving digital landscape, the ability to forge genuine human connections is more crucial than ever. Join Dr. Darren, host of Embracing Digital Transformation, for an engaging conversation with Joshua Gould, CEO of The Big Word. Together, they explore how relationship-building can be a game-changer, especially in the age of AI. ## Key Takeaways: - Emphasize interpersonal skills: Recognizing how genuine relationships boost trust can inspire professionals to value and strengthen their relationship-building skills, fostering a sense of importance and motivation. - **Hybrid working models**: The future workspace thrives on a mix of face-to-face and remote interactions. - Continuous exposure is key: Promoting a culture of personal growth through experiences like public speaking or team collaborations can make listeners feel empowered and confident in their development journey. - **Leverage unique strengths**: Businesses should capitalize on employees' distinct talents while helping them overcome weaknesses. - AI as an assistant, not a replacement: Emphasizing that humans excel at relationship-building, where AI supports but does not replace authenticity, can reassure listeners of their irreplaceable value and build confidence in their skills. - **Never stop learning**: Adaptation to AI tools is necessary, but strong interpersonal skills will always provide a competitive edge. ## Chapters: - 00:00 - Introduction to the episode and guests - 03:30 - Joshua Gould's unique background story - 08:45 - The importance of relationship-building in sales - 15:10 - The impact of COVID-19 on workplace dynamics - 22:00 - Finding the right balance between hybrid and traditional methods - 30:15 - Building trust in business and the role of AI If you found this discussion valuable and want to dive deeper, make sure to listen to the full episode of Embracing Digital Transformation and share your insights with us!
