Why Growth Strategy Needs a Reset

AI is changing how buyers discover, compare, and trust brands. Ryan Charles, a growth leader at Omnicom, makes the case that this shift is bigger than a new tool—it’s a new way to think about visibility, brand, and demand generation.

For technologists and business leaders, that matters now. Search engine optimization, paid media, social, PR, and community building are no longer separate workstreams; they’re part of one interconnected growth engine.

The New Reality of Search and Visibility

SEO Still Matters, But It’s No Longer Enough

Traditional SEO is still foundational. Clear site structure, useful content, and strong technical performance continue to matter, but AI search is changing the game by pushing more of the buyer journey into research and recommendation mode.

That means brands need to think beyond keywords. In an AI-driven world, visibility depends on whether your business is understood well enough for machines and humans to trust it.

Key takeaways

  • Good SEO gets you part of the way there.
  • AI search rewards structured content and clear brand signals.
  • Third-party mentions, reviews, and forum discussions matter more than ever.

Growth Is a System, Not a Single Channel

One of the biggest mistakes leaders make is treating growth as a siloed function. Paid, SEO, email, social, PR, and influencer marketing all influence each other, especially when the buyer has already done extensive research before reaching out.

The smarter play is holistic. Instead of asking, “Which channel should we scale?” ask, “How do these channels work together to move the customer journey forward?”

How AI Changes the Way Leaders Make Decisions

Faster Analysis, Better Questions

AI can dramatically reduce the time it takes to evaluate performance and build a growth model. What used to take hours in spreadsheets can now be done much faster, but only if you know what to ask and what to look for.

That’s the real point: AI doesn’t replace strategy. It amplifies expertise. Leaders still need judgment to decide where to invest, what assumptions to test, and when to stop a channel that isn’t performing.

Key takeaways

  • AI speeds up analysis, but human expertise still drives the decision.
  • Strong growth plans start with good questions.
  • Marketing mix modeling helps leaders understand channel impact and return.

Brand and Human Connection Still Win

As AI makes digital discovery more automated, human trust becomes more valuable. Community events, in-person activations, and local engagement create a kind of credibility that no dashboard can fake.

That’s why brands should not ignore offline touchpoints. A strong brand, real relationships, and visible community presence can create momentum that feeds back into digital performance.

Build a Flywheel, Not a Guessing Game

The future of growth belongs to organizations that connect machine-speed analysis with real-world brand building. That means using AI to move faster, but also investing in the fundamentals: product quality, reputation, and a clear message.

If your current growth model feels stuck, this is the moment to reassess what’s really working. The best brands won’t just show up in search—they’ll be recognized, recommended, and remembered.
