AI leaders urge caution as pressure rises

The AI boom is shifting from speed to discipline. Anthropic’s Dario Amodei says the industry may need to slow down and focus on safety, while mathematicians warn that smarter systems could create new risks. Europe is tightening its cyber focus as AI expands the attack surface. ENISA is tracking software vulnerabilities, frontier model risks, and the need for better code hygiene across critical systems. Two wireless fronts moved fast this week. Peraso is showcasing 60 GHz mmWave technology for advanced drone links, while Northrop Grumman and Aeronix are investing in next-generation space cryptographic systems. For technology leaders, the message is clear: connectivity and security are now inseparable, whether the mission is in the air, in space, or in the enterprise.
