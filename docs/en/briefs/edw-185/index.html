This Week at a Glance

The week of September 21, 2026 points to a clear shift: AI, cybersecurity, and cloud strategy are moving from experimentation to disciplined execution, with governments, regulators, and markets demanding stronger governance, resilience, and trust.

As Darren’s new book AI Augmented Organizations reaches the market and the ODXA standard enters public review, the signal is unmistakable: winning organizations will be designed to adapt, not just to automate.

Architect’s Take: The competitive edge now lies in execution with governance.
