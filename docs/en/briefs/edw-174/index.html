Embracing Digital This Week — Human-First AI: Trust, Security & Edge | EDTW #174

This Week at a Glance

This week in digital transformation highlights artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, edge computing, and the evolving AI-Augmented movement, with a shared emphasis on technology that supports human connection and capability.

As AI becomes more powerful, business leaders are focusing on responsible AI and cybersecurity while also using edge computing to strengthen performance. The central message is clear: the competitive advantage comes from amplifying people, not replacing them.

Architect’s Take: The strategic imperative is to align emerging technology with human outcomes.
