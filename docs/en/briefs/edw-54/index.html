Data Management

Artificial Intelligence (AI) in data management is becoming increasingly pivotal. Challenges to AI adoption include real-time data ingestion, unified data governance, and efficient data handling. However, AI has the potential to streamline data complexities, enable instant analysis, ensure governance, and optimize resource allocation for efficient decision-making. One approach to address these challenges is developing AI tools like Informatica's CLAIRE. Read More

Deloitte has teamed up with Informatica and Workiva to accelerate ESG deployments. The partnership integrates Informatica's AI-powered data management platform, Workiva's regulatory reporting offering, and Deloitte's expertise to create an integrated reporting solution. The ecosystem prioritizes adherence to regulatory requirements, transparent disclosure, and risk mitigation while aligning business practices with societal values for positive environmental contributions. The collaboration aims to address challenges identified in Deloitte's 2023 CxO Sustainability Report, such as measuring environmental impact and cost concerns. Read More

<hr>

Acceldata's latest AI technology offers DataOps teams a unique solution for customizing AI-assisted data observability to their business context. Unlike other black-box approaches, this innovative solution incorporates guardrails for business context, regulatory compliance, and the perfect balance of human oversight with AI autonomy. With an AI co-pilot, Acceldata has automated anomaly detection, cost control, rule application, and data asset description generation, making data observability simpler for enterprises. It's like having a personal assistant for your data, but way cooler! Read More

Advanced Communications

Verizon brings tech to Super Bowl LVIII with 250 mmWave 5G radios, 60 lens multibeam antennas, and 547 miles of fiber in Las Vegas. They also offer select customers a free year of NFL Sunday Ticket, ensuring an immersive experience for on-site and at-home viewers. A private 4G LTE network powers coach-to-coach communication, providing clear communication on the field. With all these technologies, even the referees can keep up with the game. Read More

<hr>

Verizon recently deployed 130,000 O-RAN capable radios and 15,000 virtualized cell sites with O-RAN compliant baseband units. This move towards Open RAN aims to increase competition and innovation in the RAN ecosystem, offering more excellent service options and encouraging new entrants in the market. Verizon's commitment to O-RAN standards reflects its dedication to driving industry advancements and delivering cutting-edge technology to customers. Read More

<hr>

BT has conducted successful trials of 5G network slicing, which allows dividing a single network into multiple ones with different characteristics. This allows customization for specific use cases, such as low-latency connections for gaming or support for massive IoT deployments. Trials demonstrate the viability of this technology, paving the way for more tailored services on BT's 5G network. Read More

Ubiquitous Computing

Reportedly, Dell and HPE have left VMware's Advantage Partner Program, launched after Broadcom's acquisition. The program aimed to simplify interactions but has faced criticism from resellers for inadequate notice and communication. The move has raised concerns among enterprise customers tied into VMware's ecosystem and could reshape the competitive landscape, with rivals looking to disrupt its longstanding enterprise connection. Read More

<hr>

The Gaia-X project, supported by Microsoft and Amazon, is in talks to resolve a dispute with the European Union. The project aims to create a European cloud platform independent of non-European providers but has faced criticism and regulatory scrutiny. Gaia-X representatives are engaging with the European Commission to address concerns and explore ways to align with EU regulations. Talks aim to find common ground and ensure compliance with EU standards. Read More
