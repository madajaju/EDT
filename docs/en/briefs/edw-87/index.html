Louisiana is set to adopt new guidelines for using artificial intelligence in schools, aiming to enhance educational outcomes while addressing safety concerns. The initiative seeks to provide educators with a framework for integrating AI responsibly into the classroom, ensuring students are well-equipped for a tech-driven future.
