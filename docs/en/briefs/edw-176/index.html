AI Demand Pushes Chips and Classrooms to Adapt

AI demand is reshaping two fronts at once: chip supply and education. TSMC’s Arizona fab is moving to expand capacity as AI workloads strain semiconductor output. At the same time, schools and universities are rewriting policies for AI in learning, grading, and support. Leaders are trying to line up talent, tools, and governance before the next wave hits. For business and digital transformation, the message is clear: infrastructure and skills must scale together.
