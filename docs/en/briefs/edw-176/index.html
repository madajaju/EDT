This Week at a Glance

AI is reshaping digital transformation on two fronts: semiconductor demand is rising as TSMC expands in Arizona, while schools and universities define clearer rules for AI use in learning and work.

The same urgency is spreading across Washington and industry, where leaders are aligning talent, infrastructure, and governance so organizations can compete in the AI-driven economy.

Architect’s Take: The signal is clear: AI readiness now depends on coordinated investment in capacity, policy, and operating rules.
