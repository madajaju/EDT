Ubiquitous Computing

Researchers are on the brink of a significant breakthrough with a copper-diamond composite that could revolutionize advanced semiconductor devices. This pioneering material, which marries diamond's exceptional thermal conductivity with copper's electrical properties, promises to revolutionize heat management and device lifespan. With the demand for high-performance electronics on the rise, this development could have far-reaching implications for semiconductor technologies, potentially sparking breakthroughs in computing and telecommunications. A new AI tool named GhostGPT has emerged, designed to assist hackers in crafting sophisticated cyberattacks. This advanced technology enables cybercriminals to automate various aspects of hacking, making it easier for them to execute attacks. Experts warn that such tools could significantly increase the threat landscape, as even less skilled hackers can now access enhanced capabilities for malicious activities. Read More

The EU's Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) is set to impose stringent cybersecurity requirements on banks, but the industry is alarmingly unprepared for compliance. Initial assessments reveal that numerous banks lack the necessary technical and organizational measures to meet the new standards. The legislation, designed to fortify the financial sector against cyber threats, underscores the urgent need to enhance security infrastructures across the industry. Read More
