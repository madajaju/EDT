This Week at a Glance

This week’s digital transformation coverage centers on the shift from AI hype to practical execution. Companies including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Ford are showing that smart tools still require seasoned human judgment.

The broader message across cybersecurity and data management is consistent: organizations that combine stronger defenses, cleaner data, and experienced people will move faster, build trust, and strengthen resilience in the next phase of digital transformation.
