AI-Driven Attacks Target Latin American Institutions

AI-augmented cyberattacks are escalating across Latin America. For leaders, the message is clear: security, training, and governance must evolve as fast as the technology itself.

Cyberattacks Push Utilities Into Faster Defense

Cyberattacks against Minnesota water utilities disrupted operational technology and triggered a multi-agency response. The incident shows how critical infrastructure remains a high-value target as attackers press into systems that keep essential services running. Security teams must coordinate across IT, OT, and public agencies to reduce exposure and recover faster. So what: digital resilience is now a business continuity issue, not just a technical one, for every organization with connected operations.
