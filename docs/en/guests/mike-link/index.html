Mike Link

Biography

Mike Link is a dynamic operations and business development executive specializing in health technology, computer vision AI, and eldercare innovation. He currently serves as the Chief Operating Officer (COO) of KamiCare, a specialized division of Kami Vision that leverages advanced Vision AI and edge computing to provide private, non-wearable fall detection for senior living communities and memory care facilities.Throughout his career, Mike has been at the forefront of scaling artificial intelligence solutions to address real-world health and safety challenges. Prior to stepping into his role as COO, he served as the Vice President of Sales for Security & Healthcare at Kami Vision, where he directed go-to-market strategies and commercial operations. A recognized thought leader in the aging-in-place sector, he has represented the company on high-profile industry platforms, including presenting on smart home universal standards for senior safety at CES.Before joining Kami Vision, Mike honed his industry expertise at SafelyYou as an Account Executive and Fall Prevention Specialist, driving the adoption of technology aimed at reducing injuries among vulnerable populations. His deep-rooted passion for neurological health and elderly care began during his time at the University of Redlands, where he graduated with a degree in Neuroscience and Entrepreneurship while simultaneously serving as a campus chapter president for the Youth Movement Against Alzheimer's.Today, Mike bridges the gap between complex AI capabilities and compassionate care, ensuring that seniors can maintain their independence and dignity through cutting-edge technological support.
