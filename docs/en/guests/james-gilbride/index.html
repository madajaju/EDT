James Gilbride

Biography

James Gilbride stewards DFS’s administrative and technology divisions: information technology, security, and application development. Ensuring the predictability, accountability, integrity, and protection of your data – from point of origin to print floor. He brings a people-centric approach to leadership, responsible for customer onboarding and ongoing interactions to account services and human resources. He sees DFS as a culture that cares for our customers and employees over everything else.

James says, “I have a singular interest in being everything to everyone: customers, employees, and shareholders. I have the capability to expedite decision-making and collaboration while creating ease in business. Supporting a team of confident people willing to try new things to get light years ahead. We work through everything together. I am always striving for simplicity and peace of mind for the end user.”
