Biography

Legoax is an SEO agency specializing in SEO strategy and link building for French and international clients. We combine technical expertise and performance to support our clients in competitive sectors such as e-commerce, local services, and complex niches like online casinos. Our areas of expertise: 1. SEO Strategy: In-depth analysis, prioritization, and creation of a customized roadmap to improve our clients' visibility. 2. Link Building: Management of campaigns starting at €2,000/month, to position our clients on highly competitive keywords. 3. Content Marketing: Production and optimization of content, combining human expertise and innovative tools to maximize ROI. 4. Training and Consulting: Personalized sessions to train teams or support leaders on best SEO practices. We have already supported recognized brands such as Interflora, R-PUR, Selectra, and SNB Technologies, generating concrete results: Increase in SEO visibility of up to 40% within the first month. About me: I am Léo Poitevin, founder of Legoax. After several years managing ambitious SEO projects for companies like Green Tomato Media and Techopedia, I decided to create my own structure to offer specialized expertise to my clients. Passionate about performance and innovation, I regularly speak at conferences to share my experiences on SEO and link building. In parallel, I manage a YouTube channel where I share SEO tips and strategies, followed by a growing community of web professionals. Our values: Measurable performance: Every action aims to generate a direct impact on our clients' KPIs. Continuous innovation: Regular testing of new methods and tools to stay at the forefront. Trusted partnership: Complete transparency in our approaches and results.
